**Welcome!**

I'm a postdoc in the [Lo Lab](https://med.stanford.edu/lo-lab.html) at Stanford School of Medicine. I build mathematical and statistical models to better understand infectious disease dynamics, evaluate the effectiveness of interventions, and inform data-driven policy recommendations. I'm broadly interested in respiratory and vaccine-preventable diseases. 

I earned my PhD in Computer Science from CU Boulder working with [Dan Larremore](https://larremorelab.github.io/) as part of the BioFrontiers Institute's Interdisciplinary Quantitative Biology program, where I developed population- and individual-level mechanistic models of disease spread to inform vaccine prioritiziation, routine testing programs, and reactive testing programs at airports to aid in the COVID-19 pandemic response.

My research has been published in scientific journals including Science, JAMA, eLife, and Nature Communications, and covered by outlets such as the [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/opinions/2026/04/28/america-could-lose-important-measles-status-vaccine-trust-erodes/), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2021/01/10/955384322/vaccine-strategies), and the [Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/health/healthcare/should-people-who-have-had-covid-19-wait-to-get-a-vaccine-11608562810). It has also been cited by the [WHO](https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/845a606d-27f3-4efb-8971-44fd5cfb7fdc/content).
