**Welcome!**

I'm a postdoc in the [Lo Lab](https://med.stanford.edu/lo-lab.html) at Stanford School of Medicine. I build mathematical and statistical models to better understand infectious disease dynamics, evaluate interventions, and inform public health policy. I'm broadly interested in respiratory and vaccine-preventable diseases. I earned my PhD in Computer Science from CU Boulder working with [Dan Larremore](https://larremorelab.github.io/) as part of the BioFrontiers Institute's Interdisciplinary Quantitative Biology program, where I developed population- and individual-level mechanistic models of disease spread to inform vaccine priotiziation, routine testing programs, and reactionary testing programs at airports to aid in the COVID-19 pandemic response.
